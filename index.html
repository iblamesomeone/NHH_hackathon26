Core AI Formula
RI = ( P / 32 ) × T × Fmo × Fsh + S
P — Pressure in mmHg
T — Time immobility multiplier
Fmo — Moisture × temperature factor
Fsh — Shear factor from bed angle
S — Systemic medical score
RI < 10 = Low risk
RI 10–25 = Moderate risk
RI 25–50 = High risk
RI ≥ 50 = Very High / Critical
