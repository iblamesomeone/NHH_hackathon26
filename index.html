HAPU Risk Model — Formula ReferenceHAPU जोखिम मॉडल

Risk Score = softCap(B × P × T × M × H), where softCap(r) = 5r/(r+4) maps raw value to 0–5 scale.

B — Braden Vulnerability(23−score)/17
P — Pressure Multiplier1.0–2.0×
T — Time Multiplier1.0–2.5×
M — Moisture Multiplier1.0–1.4×
H — Shear Multiplier1.0–1.625×
Braden Scale6–23 (lower = worse)
